Canbury Gardens sits right on the Thames in Kingston and has a wonderfully laid-back, neighbourhood feel that puts families and couples immediately at ease. The park stretches along the riverbank with mature trees, open lawns, and well-kept flowerbeds — the kind of green space that feels genuinely local rather than manicured and formal.

Spring is the prime season here, when the cherry trees and ornamental shrubs burst into blossom and the afternoon light falls through the branches in soft, glowing pools. Summer evenings are equally beautiful — long golden light reflecting off the river, families and couples relaxed and unhurried, the gentle sound of the water adding to the mood.

It's a wonderfully accessible location: easy parking, close to Kingston town centre, and relaxed enough that children can wander and explore while we capture natural, unposed moments. If you're based in or around Kingston and want a session that feels close to home without sacrificing beauty, Canbury Gardens is an excellent choice.

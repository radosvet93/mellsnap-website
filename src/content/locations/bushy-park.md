Bushy Park is Richmond Park's quieter, slightly less famous neighbour — and for that reason, it often makes for a more relaxed and personal shoot experience. Covering over 1,000 acres between Teddington and Hampton Court, it shares much of the same wild parkland character: ancient trees, open meadow, resident deer, and a tangible sense of natural space that feels miles from the city.

The park's centrepiece is the magnificent avenue of horse chestnut trees stretching from Teddington Gate to the Diana Fountain — in late spring it's lined with white candles of blossom, and in autumn it blazes gold and copper. Around the fountain itself, formal water gardens and open grassland provide a more structured backdrop for those who want something a little more composed alongside the wildness.

For families with young children, Bushy Park is hard to beat. The flat, open terrain means everyone can spread out and move freely, the deer and waterfowl give children something to be genuinely excited about, and the variety of woodland and meadow means there's always a fresh backdrop around the next bend. We shoot here year-round and always find something beautiful to work with, whatever the season.

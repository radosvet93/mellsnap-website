Terrace Garden is one of Richmond's hidden gems — a formally terraced hillside garden tucked below Richmond Hill that most people walk past without realising quite how beautiful it is. Carved into the slope above the Thames, it offers a series of descending terraced lawns and formal beds that frame extraordinary views over the river and the Petersham meadows beyond. On a clear summer afternoon with the sun lowering toward the west, this view is simply spectacular.

The garden itself is well-maintained and genuinely varied. In spring, wisteria drapes over the pergola walks in heavy purple swags, and flowering borders add soft colour to the stone pathways. In summer, the formal beds are in full bloom and the Thames views are at their most vivid — golden light playing across the water and the broad green meadows of Ham. Autumn brings a gentle, mellow quality to the light and a warmth to the tones that suits both couples and family portraits.

It's a relatively compact location but one that offers real variety — formal garden, riverside panorama, wooded paths — within a short walking distance. For couples in particular, the elevated terraces looking out over the Thames are among the most romantic backdrops we have access to anywhere in SW London.

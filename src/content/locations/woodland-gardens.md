The woodland sections of Richmond Park — away from the open meadow and the main deer grasslands — offer a very different photographic experience from the park's better-known vistas. Under the canopy of ancient oaks and sweet chestnuts, the world becomes quieter, greener, and more intimate. The light is softer and more complex, filtered through layers of leaf and branch into pools and streaks that create beautiful, painterly conditions for portrait photography.

In spring, these woodland areas come alive with ground-level colour. Wild garlic carpets the shadier spots with clouds of white blossom and a heady scent; bluebells appear in sheltered clearings; and the new foliage glows an almost luminous green in the April light. It's a genuinely enchanting environment that photographs with a quality more reminiscent of a fairy tale than a London park.

Autumn transforms the same spaces into something richer and warmer. The canopy turns gold and amber, fallen leaves carpet the paths, and shafts of late-season sunlight pierce through the branches at low angles. For maternity photography in particular, this warm, sheltered woodland environment — with its sense of quiet and natural abundance — creates images of real emotional depth.

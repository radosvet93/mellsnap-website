Richmond Park is one of London's great natural treasures — 2,500 acres of ancient parkland, woodland, and open meadow just a short drive from SW London. For photography, it offers an almost unmatched variety of backdrops across every season. In spring, fresh green bracken and wildflowers carpet the ground; summer brings long golden evenings and lush canopies; autumn transforms the park into a tapestry of amber, rust, and gold; and even winter has its own quiet magic, with low mist over the ponds and stark oak silhouettes against pale skies.

What makes Richmond Park truly special for family and portrait shoots is the sense of space and wildness that's rare so close to the city. You can find complete solitude among the ancient oaks, or frame a family against sweeping open meadows with London barely visible on the horizon. The 650 resident deer add an extraordinary natural element — not staged or scripted, just part of the landscape, often drifting softly through the background of an image.

Whether you're after relaxed lifestyle portraits of your children playing in the bracken, intimate maternity images among the wildflowers, or romantic couples shots at golden hour with the Thames Valley glowing in the distance from Pembroke Lodge, Richmond Park delivers. It's our most-booked location for good reason.

Autumn is hands down my favourite season for family photography. The golden light, colourful leaves, and cosy atmosphere create the perfect backdrop for beautiful family portraits. If you're planning an autumn family photoshoot in London, this guide has everything you need to know.

## Why Autumn is Perfect for Family Photos

The autumn months offer some of the most beautiful conditions for photography:

- **Golden light**: The sun sits lower in the sky, creating warm, soft light throughout the day
- **Stunning colours**: Red, orange, gold, and brown leaves provide a vibrant natural backdrop
- **Comfortable weather**: Not too hot, not too cold – perfect for outdoor sessions
- **Natural props**: Fallen leaves, conkers, and autumn flowers add interest to your photos

## Best Time for Autumn Photos in London

The peak autumn colour in London typically occurs from mid-October to early November. However, the timing varies each year depending on the weather. I recommend booking your session for late October to catch the leaves at their most colourful.

## Best Locations for Autumn Photoshoots

### Richmond Park

Richmond Park is stunning in autumn. The ancient oak trees, open spaces, and roaming deer create a magical atmosphere. The Isabella Plantation is particularly beautiful, with its Japanese maples turning brilliant shades of red.

### Bushy Park

The avenue of chestnut trees at Bushy Park is iconic in autumn. The fallen leaves create a golden carpet, perfect for playful family shots.

### Greenwich Park

With panoramic views of London and beautiful tree-lined avenues, Greenwich Park offers diverse autumn backdrops in one location.

### Kew Gardens

While there's an entry fee, Kew Gardens offers unparalleled autumn beauty. The treetop walkway and woodland areas are particularly stunning.

## What to Wear for Autumn Photos

Autumn calls for warm, earthy tones that complement the season:

- **Colours**: Burgundy, mustard, rust, olive green, cream, and brown
- **Layers**: Cardigans, scarves, and jackets add visual interest
- **Textures**: Knits, corduroy, and denim photograph beautifully
- **Avoid**: Bright colours that clash with the autumn palette

### Outfit Coordination Tips

- Choose a colour palette of 2-3 complementary colours
- Coordinate outfits without being too matchy-matchy
- Make sure everyone is comfortable and can move freely
- Consider the location when choosing colours

## Preparing for Your Autumn Session

### Weather Considerations

British autumn weather can be unpredictable. Come prepared with:

- Layers that can be added or removed
- Waterproof jackets just in case
- Wellies for the kids (they can look great in photos!)
- A blanket for sitting on damp ground

### Timing

I recommend booking sessions for late afternoon to catch the golden hour. The light during this time is warm and flattering, and it coincides perfectly with the autumn sun.

### For Families with Children

- Schedule around nap times
- Bring snacks and drinks
- Embrace the mess – playing in leaves makes great photos!
- Dress kids in clothes you don't mind getting dirty

## Autumn Photo Session Ideas

- Throwing leaves in the air
- Collecting conkers and autumn treasures
- Cosy blanket moments
- Walking hand in hand through the leaves
- Playing hide and seek behind trees
- Cuddling on a bench surrounded by autumn colours

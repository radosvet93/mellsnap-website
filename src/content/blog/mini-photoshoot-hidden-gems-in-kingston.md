Kingston upon Thames is full of beautiful locations perfect for a mini photoshoot. While the well-known spots like Bushy Park and the riverside are lovely, there are some hidden gems that can make your photos truly special. Here are my favourite secret spots in Kingston for stunning mini photoshoot sessions.

## Canbury Gardens

Canbury Gardens is a peaceful riverside park that often gets overlooked in favour of busier spots. The beautiful Victorian bandstand provides a charming backdrop, while the tree-lined paths and views of the Thames create a serene setting. It's particularly beautiful in spring when the flowers are blooming.

## Kingston Cemetery

This might sound unusual, but Kingston Cemetery is actually a stunning Victorian garden cemetery with beautiful architecture, old trees, and peaceful pathways. The ornate monuments and mature landscaping create a unique and atmospheric backdrop for photos. Just be respectful and quiet while visiting.

## Fairfield Recreation Ground

Just a short walk from the town centre, Fairfield offers beautiful open spaces, mature trees, and lovely light throughout the day. It's less crowded than the main parks but equally photogenic. The old bandstand and surrounding trees are particularly nice for portraits.

## The Ancient Market Place

The historic Market Place in Kingston town centre has beautiful Georgian architecture and charming cobbled areas. Early morning is the best time to visit when it's quiet, and you can capture the beautiful buildings without crowds.

## Hogsmill Open Space

Following the Hogsmill River through Kingston, you'll find pockets of wild, natural beauty that feel miles away from the town. The riverside paths, wildflowers, and wooden bridges create a rustic, romantic setting perfect for couples and families alike.

## Ham House Gardens

Just a short distance from Kingston, Ham House boasts stunning 17th-century gardens that have been beautifully preserved. The formal gardens, lavender-lined paths, and historic house create an elegant backdrop. Entry to the gardens is free.

## Kingston University Campus

The newer parts of Kingston University campus feature modern architecture with interesting angles and textures. The buildings along the river make for striking contemporary backdrops, especially for professional portraits.

## Tips for Your Kingston Mini Photoshoot

- **Timing matters**: Early morning or late afternoon provides the best light and fewer crowds
- **Weekdays are quieter**: If possible, schedule your session on a weekday for more privacy
- **Walk and explore**: Kingston's compact size means we can easily visit multiple spots in one session
- **Be weather-ready**: British weather is unpredictable, but overcast days often provide beautiful, soft light
